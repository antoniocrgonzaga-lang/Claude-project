CONTROLS

Ship

  • W/S throttle up / brake
  • Mouse or A/D + ↑/↓ steer
  • Q/E roll · Space/C rise / sink
  • Shift boost (burns fuel) · L land / take off

On foot

  • WASD move · Shift run · Space jump
  • Mouse look · hold E harvest / capture
  • hold X next to a building to dismantle it (refunds part of its cost)
  • F exit / board ship · B build mode
  • G ship locator: walk automatically to your ship (G or WASD to stop)

Build mode

  • 1–9 choose building · R rotate · click to place
  • Click a building to select · right-click / Esc cancel
  • WASD pan · Q/E or right-drag rotate · wheel zoom · B exit

Units (in build mode)

  • Click a unit or drag a box to select · Shift adds
  • Right-click: ground = move · resource = gather · unfinished / damaged building = build / repair · enemy = attack
  • Train units by selecting a Barracks, Robotics Factory or Hangar

General

  • H toggle help · Esc pause · U evolve the base AI when ready
  • M Tech Moon panel (after the ASI tier): build, travel, automation
  • N summon the Tech Moon to the planet you are on / near
  • K mute / unmute sound · volumes in the pause menu (Esc)
  • No pointer lock? Hold a mouse button and drag to look.
